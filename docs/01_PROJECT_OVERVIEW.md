# 🛍️ Project Overview

## Project Name

Fashion E-Commerce Platform

Version: 1.0.0

Status: Planning Phase

---

# 1. Introduction

This project aims to build a modern, scalable, secure and responsive e-commerce platform for a local fashion business specializing in women's ethnic wear such as Kurtis, Salwar Suits, Co-ord Sets, Dupattas and other fashion accessories.

The website will serve as the brand's primary online shopping platform, allowing customers to browse products, place orders and securely make payments while giving the business owner complete control through an Admin Dashboard.

This platform is being designed as a reusable codebase that can later be customized for other fashion businesses with minimal changes.

---

# 2. Business Objective

The client already has an established audience on Instagram and wants to convert social media visitors into paying customers through a professional shopping experience.

The website should:

• Build trust

• Increase online sales

• Improve customer experience

• Reduce dependency on Instagram DMs

• Provide easy product management

• Allow future business expansion
